About Raju traders

Committed to Pure Vegetarian Excellence & Unmatched Taste

Established in 2024 by Shri Raju Yadav, Raju traders has quickly become Newada Lawa’s destination for wholesome vegetarian dining and handcrafted sweets. We believe great food starts with authentic ingredients and honest preparation.

Whether you are stopping by for breakfast chai and samosas, sharing an evening dinner thali with family, or picking up festive boxes of fresh laddoos and gulab jamun, we guarantee authentic flavours, spotless hygiene, and courteous service.
